# LinkedIn profile — rewrite

## The problem being fixed

The current About says:

> I shut down my last venture, Venditas, a year in, not because it failed, but
> because I realized the problem wasn't real.

We are selling under the name Venditas. Twenty-five UK recruitment agencies were
emailed from `founder@venditas.in` today. A recruiter who looks up the sender
finds the founder saying he shut that company down.

There are only two honest ways out, and picking one is the founder's call.

**Option A — reclaim the name.** Say plainly that Venditas came back for a
different problem. This is true, it demonstrates the judgement he is already
selling on that profile, and it costs nothing. Recommended.

**Option B — drop the name.** Rebrand the product, buy a new domain, and leave
the About section alone. Cleaner separation, but it throws away a live site,
authenticated email, and a domain with a working sending reputation — which took
most of a day to establish.

Everything below assumes Option A.

---

## Headline

The current one is aimed at people hiring an AI engineer. That is a different
audience from a recruitment agency owner deciding whether to accept a
connection, and the headline is nearly all they see.

**If LinkedIn is being used mainly for Venditas outreach:**

> Building Venditas — candidate CVs in your agency's template, contact details
> stripped | Previously: 50 users in 14 days on Hustlr

*169 characters.* Leads with what a recruiter can immediately place, keeps the
proof point that makes him worth accepting.

**If the profile has to serve both Venditas and engineering work:**

> Full-Stack AI Engineer | Building Venditas: branded, redacted CVs for
> recruitment agencies | 50 users in 14 days on Hustlr

*127 characters.* Weaker for recruiters, honest about the split.

### What changed, and why

**The Venditas line now works for us.** "I shut it down, then restarted it
against a problem I could verify" demonstrates exactly the judgement the profile
is already claiming. Left as-is it says the opposite about the company currently
emailing people.

**Venditas moved to the top.** It was buried under Hustlr and Labs60. A
recruiter who accepts a connection needs to reach the relevant thing in the
first two lines.

**"I realized the problem wasn't real" became specific.** The original is a
claim about judgement; the replacement shows the working — evidence found, or
not found. Same point, and checkable.

**A URL and an offer.** The old About ends by asking for inbound engineering
work. For this audience it should end with something they can do in ten seconds.

## Before connecting to anyone

Three things a recruitment agency owner checks in the two seconds a connection
request is on screen:

1. **A real photo.** Not optional with this audience.
2. **A headline they can place.** "Founder" tells them nothing; the CV line
   tells them whether to care.
3. **Consistency with the email.** They received mail from Venditas. If the
   profile does not obviously connect to it, the request looks unrelated at
   best and fabricated at worst.
