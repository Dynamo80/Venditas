# 005 — UK agencies, and the CRMs to avoid

**Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Status:** active

## Decision

Target **UK and Ireland recruitment agencies, 3–50 staff**. Not US in-house
talent teams. Screen out agencies running a CRM that already does this.

## The segmentation finding

From public recruiter discussions, the split is sharp and consistent:

- Every "this eats my week" voice is a **UK or EU agency submitting candidates
  under a client template**.
- Every "this is a non-issue" voice is a **US in-house or corporate talent
  team**, who have no client to submit to.

The market is narrower than "recruiters" and pretending otherwise wastes
outreach.

## CRMs that already ship this — do not pitch them

| CRM | Native branded formatting | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Loxo | Yes, **free** | One-click anonymise. The dangerous one |
| Recruit CRM | Yes | Business tier, overlays on the original |
| Zoho Recruit | Yes | All paid Staffing editions |
| Vincere | Yes | Rebuilds from CRM fields |
| **Bullhorn** | **No** | Largest installed base — the beachhead |
| **JobAdder** | **No** | Marketplace add-ons only |
| **Mercury** | **No** | |

Bullhorn agencies are the priority: biggest base, no native feature.

## Corroboration that the work is real

- Live UK job adverts list "formatting CVs" as a paid duty in recruitment
  coordinator and consultant roles at £26–35k.
- Offshore providers sell CV formatting at $1.50–2.50 per CV; a UK human service
  charges £1.95 a page. That also sets a low price ceiling.
