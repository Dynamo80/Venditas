# 016 — Growth is paid for by growth

**Date:** 2026-09-13 · **Status:** active
**Decided by:** the founder, answering the seven questions in `docs/strategy-10k.md`
**Amends:** [007](007-invoice-not-checkout.md) on when invoicing ends
**Delegates:** markets, integrations and partnerships to [017](017-markets-integrations-partnerships.md)

## Decision

- **Budget stays at $0.** Nothing is spent until money has come in, and then only
  out of that money.
- **Payment:** invoices through Skydo (007) for the first one or two paying
  agencies. After that, a Paddle checkout, set up and paid for out of what
  those agencies paid.
- **Sending:** venditas.in only, at 25 a day. No second sending domain for now.
- **Hustlr and Labs60 are never used for Venditas.** Not as a channel, not as a
  list of people to contact, not as credibility, and not mentioned in any
  Venditas material, LinkedIn included.

## Why

The business was built on the rule that it spends nothing. A channel should
prove itself with revenue before money goes into making it bigger. The founder's
other ventures stay separate from this one, without exception.

## What it costs

The strategy's base case moves from month 13 to **month 14 (November 2027)**.
Paid channels now start only after the revenue that pays for them arrives: a
second sending domain at about five agencies, TRN at about £1,000 MRR. The
model is in `docs/strategy-10k.md`.

## Rejected

- **Up to £340 a month before the first customer,** as proposed in
  `docs/strategy-10k.md`. The founder rejected spending ahead of revenue.
- **Five secondary sending domains now.** They can be revisited once revenue
  can pay for them (017, rung 2).
- **One message to Hustlr's users.** Ruled out permanently.
