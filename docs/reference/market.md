# Market evidence

Researched 2026-09-02. Sources are public and dated; the weak ones are labelled.

## The pain is real — the good evidence

**UK job adverts list it as a paid duty.** Live on Reed: Recruitment Coordinator
(£26–30k) "formatting CVs to a professional standard"; Trainee Recruitment
Consultant (£26k) lists "formatting CVs" first among learned tasks; others at
£28–40k. Modest volume — it is a line item inside a role, not a job in itself.

**People sell it as a standalone priced service.** Offshore at $1.50–2.50 per
CV; a UK human service at £1.95 a page, £12 minimum. Offshore back-office teams
at $1,350/month per person. **This also sets a low price ceiling.**

**Recruiters describe the exact workflow unprompted.** One thread pairs
reformatting with redaction without being asked: "we remove all of the contact
information and add our company information." Another documents a firm moving
from hand-formatting to paying for HireAra.

**The best single artefact for the pitch:** a candidate discovered his recruiter
had stripped his contact details by hand and accidentally deleted his degree
along with them, which he then had to explain in interviews. It documents both
the practice and the failure mode of doing it manually.

## What is weak, and must not be quoted as fact

**Every "15–45 minutes per CV" figure traces to a vendor's own survey.** The
trade press coverage is entirely supplier-submitted content — The Global
Recruiter's whole CV-formatting archive is categorised "Supplier Blogs". There
is no independent measurement. **Do not put it in a pitch as an industry
statistic.**

**Redaction prevalence is unquantified.** Practitioners do it, practitioners
refuse it, one client demanded the opposite, vendors sell it. No survey.
"Most agencies redact" is unproven.

## Market size

UK, ONS 2025: **31,345** recruitment enterprises, **92.9% under 50 staff**,
77.9% under £1m turnover. The target segment is the overwhelming majority of the
market by count.

A UK placement fee at median salary is roughly **£5,900–9,800**. A year of
Venditas at £79 is about **12% of one fee**.

## The headwind

Staffing Hub 2026: **69% of agencies are holding technology spend flat, 6% are
cutting.** New spend has to displace something.
