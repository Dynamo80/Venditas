# Competitors

Researched 2026-09-02. Prices read first-hand from vendor pages unless marked.

## The one that matters

**HireAra** — £180 / £450 / £950 per month + VAT, unlimited users, capped by
candidates per year (1,500 / 5,000 / 12,000). UK, agency-focused, acquired by
The Access Group in October 2024. Notably makes **no mention of redaction**,
which is either an opportunity or a signal that it does not sell.

## The floor — the anchoring problem

A cluster of micro-competitors has trained buyers to expect very little:

| | Price |
|---|---|
| FormaCV | $0.99 per CV |
| CVReady | $0.95 per CV |
| RefineCV | $0.40 per CV, or $50/mo for 200 |
| RemakeCV | $25 per user/month |
| CVFormatter | $79 for 100 credits |
| Saply | €50 per seat |

Several advertise "no per-seat fees". A prospect who has seen these arrives
expecting £50, not £149.

## Quote-only

Allsorter (Bullhorn Marketplace partner), Daxtra Styler, Odro AssistCV, Kyloe,
RChilli.

## The warning

**Quibench** — a UK CV formatting *and redaction* tool aimed at recruitment
agencies — shut down in August 2026. Its site reads "no longer operating under
this brand". Closest possible comparable, and it died.

## The real competitor

A recruiter with ChatGPT and a Word template. Quoted publicly: *"Create your own
GPT. Take off names and phone numbers. Fuck buying something off the shelf."*

Our answer: it produces a Word document in their actual template, it verifies
the redaction, and it does not silently improve a candidate's achievements —
which is exactly what a general chatbot does when asked to tidy a CV, and
exactly what cannot be sent to a client.

## CRMs shipping this natively

See `docs/decisions/005-target-market.md`. Short version: Loxo (free), Recruit
CRM, Zoho Recruit and Vincere have it. Bullhorn, JobAdder and Mercury do not —
those are the prospects.
