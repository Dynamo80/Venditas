# Growth research — what reaches £10,000 MRR

**Date:** 13 September 2026. Six research threads ran in parallel, and every
one ran out of web searches before finishing, so each notes what it could not
find. The strategy built on this is in `docs/strategy-10k.md`.

Tags: **[V]** checked on the vendor's or other primary page · **[2H]** second-hand ·
**[I]** our inference. Nothing tagged [2H] or [I] should go into a pitch
as a fact.

**What it takes to get listed:**

- **Bullhorn**
  - Cost: annual platform fee, reported as $5,000 [2H], plus **$1K–4K validation** [V].
  - Requirements: a contract and a security review. "We do not offer free access to anybody" [V]. Reportedly asks AI vendors for two Bullhorn customers first [2H].
  - Bullhorn's own guide says "most partners don't rely heavily on this listing for lead generation" ([guide](https://www.bullhorn.com/partner-program-guide/bullhorn-platform-partner-program/)) [V].
  - Formatting tools already listed: Allsorter, Daxtra, Kyloe.
- **JobAdder**
  - Cost: none published. Free developer account; the app must be approved in writing.
  - The API terms forbid competing with JobAdder and use NSW (Australian) law ([terms](https://jobadder.com/api-terms/)) [V].
  - Formatting tools already listed: CVFormatter, CV-Transformer, Allsorter, each writing the result back to the candidate record ([listing](https://jobadder.com/integration/cv-formatter/)) [V].
- **Firefish**
  - Cost: "We do not charge anything to join" [V].
  - Access: an agency admin can create API credentials themselves ([help](https://fishtank.firefishsoftware.com/custom-api-settings)) [V].
  - Partners get co-marketing to Firefish users [V].
  - Listed: 50+ partners, **none of them CV formatting** ([marketplace](https://www.firefishsoftware.com/product/integrations)) [V].
  - A customer asked for a formatting integration in June 2025 ([uservoice](https://firefishsoftware.uservoice.com/forums/194828-firefish-customer-ideas/suggestions/50069292-integration-with-cv-transformer)) [V].
  - **Not confirmed:** whether the API can read a CV file and write a document back.
- **Vincere:** API keys only with a formal partner agreement ([help](http://help.vincere.io/en/articles/9618701-understanding-and-requesting-api-keys)) [V]. The Access Group owns both Vincere and HireAra.
- **Tracker and Itris:** open APIs; share of small UK agencies unknown ([Tracker](https://www.tracker-rms.com/press-releases/tracker-open-api-recruitment-agencies-control/), [Itris](https://www.itris.co.uk/partners/)) [V].
- **Microsoft AppSource:** free to list ([MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/marketplace-offers/appsource-submission-faq)) [V].
- **Chrome Web Store:** one-time developer fee ([docs](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/register)) [V]; $5 [2H].

**Evidence that a listing brings customers: none found.** No install counts, and no
competitor says where its customers came from.

## 2. How competitors grew

### HireAra
- **Origin:** spun out of DigiBlu in April 2022; company no. 14030842 ([Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/14030842/filing-history)) [V].
- **Seed round, January 2024:** €527K ([Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2024/01/31/hireara-raises-eur527k-seed-funding/)) [2H].
  - 55% came from five existing customers and founders of Mercury, SourceWhale and Candidate.ID.
  - The money went into tripling the sales team.
- **Integrations:** 8 CRMs ([fact sheet](https://www.hireara.ai/about-hireara)) [V].
- **Sales model:** demo first, no trial [2H].
- **Size:** estimated $1.8M ARR and 16 staff ([Getlatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/hireara.ai)) [2H, estimate].
- **Acquisition:** Access UK Ltd took control on 29 October 2024 [V].
- **Price:** unchanged at £180 / £450 / £950 ([pricing](https://www.hireara.ai/pricing)) [V].
- **Correction:** Access's page says "Anonymise CVs for compliant submissions" ([Access](https://www.theaccessgroup.com/en-gb/products/hireara/)) [V]. Our note that HireAra does not mention redaction was wrong.
- **Named case-study agencies:** Realtime Recruitment, Puro Associates, Rotherwood Recruitment, Timberseed, Bruin, Caval [V].
- **Trustpilot:** rating suspended for a breach of guidelines; 2 of 96 reviews are from the last 12 months ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hireara.ai)) [V].

### Quibench
- **Who:** a Polish sole trader, founder Kiryl Rusanau ([archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20260705222231/https://quibench.io/about)) [V].
- **Price:** €29/month for 50 CVs, €99 for 200 ([archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20260705222218/https://quibench.io/)) [V].
- **How it sold:** self-serve, driven by content and comparison pages.
- **Customers:** **no named customer found anywhere.**
- **History:** pivoted from interview preparation in 2025; shut down 11 August 2026. The customer data is deleted, so there is nothing to acquire [V].

**What customers complain about most: invented or scrambled content. Nobody
complains about price in public.**
- HireAra: "any time saved during parsing is completely lost on checking for errors/hallucinations" (November 2025, [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hireara.ai)) [V]
- Allsorter: "inaccurate dates, mis-format information and change[d] the order of time sequences" (April 2026, [Trustpilot](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/allsorter.com)) [V]
- Daxtra: "better for us to just manually format our resumes" ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/133344/Daxtra/reviews/)) [V]

**Buying a competitor instead.** Nothing relevant is listed on Flippa [V]. Micro-SaaS sells
at a median 3.9× annual profit ([Acquire.com, January 2026](https://blog.acquire.com/acquire-com-biannual-acquisition-multiples-report-jan-2026/)) [V].

## 5. Outbound volume, benchmarks, payments

**Benchmarks:**
- Cold email: **0.45% reply rate per email**, from 7.5M emails in 2025 ([Belkins](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates)) [V].
- Trial to paid, no card: good 4–6%, great 10–15%. With a card: good 25–35% ([ChartMogul, February 2026](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/)) [V].
- Small-business SaaS churn: 3–7% a month ([SaaS Flywheel](https://saasflywheel.io/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks-2026)) [2H].
- LinkedIn: 27.1% of invitations accepted; 27.6% of those accepted reply ([Reachium](https://www.reachium.io/research/linkedin-outreach-benchmarks-2026)) [V].
- **Our own numbers so far:** 54 sends × 0.45% = 0.24 expected replies, so zero replies tells us nothing yet [I].
- **Scale needed:** 0.25–0.9 customers per 1,000 prospects contacted, so 77 customers needs roughly 85k–300k prospects [I].

**Sending setup at scale:**
- Smartlead's Zapmail mailboxes: $4.50/mailbox/month, domains $13/year ([Smartlead](https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing)) [V].
- Instantly Growth: $47/month ([Instantly](https://instantly.ai/pricing)) [V].
- Realistic volume: 5 domains × 3 mailboxes × 20/day ≈ 300/day after a 2–3 week warm-up [2H/I].
- Cost: about £90/month [I].

**UK law on B2B cold email (PECR):**
- Limited companies, LLPs and plcs are corporate subscribers and can be emailed with an opt-out. Sole traders and non-LLP partnerships need consent ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)) [2H].
- The maximum fine is now £17.5m or 4% of turnover, from 5 February 2026 ([Clifford Chance](https://www.cliffordchance.com/insights/resources/blogs/talking-tech/en/articles/2026/02/key-aspects-of-the-data--use-and-access--act-take-effect.html)) [V].
- `ops/build-prospects.mjs` already keeps LIMITED/LTD/LLP only.

**Payments:**
- **Skydo:** $19 per transaction up to $2,000, plus GST ([Skydo](https://www.skydo.com/faqs/fees?q=how-much-does-skydo-charge)) [V]. That is about 18% of a £79 payment [I].
- **Paddle:** 5% + 50¢, no monthly fee, merchant of record ([pricing](https://www.paddle.com/pricing)) [V].
  - India is not on the excluded list, and sole proprietors are accepted ([countries](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle)) [V].
  - The review takes 5–7 business days [V].
- **Dodo Payments:** about 6% + 40¢ for international subscriptions; built for Indian founders ([pricing](https://dodopayments.com/pricing)) [V].
- **Polar:** India listed for payouts ([countries](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries)) [V].
- **Stripe Managed Payments:** India not eligible ([Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments/eligibility)) [V].

## 6. Search, directories, AI answers

- **Search volume.** None measured: Google Trends, Similarweb and SpyFu all blocked the research.
  - Google UK autocomplete confirms people search: "cv formatting software for recruiters", "anonymise cv tool", "blind cv template word", "redact cv online", "remove personal details from cv" [V].
  - **Nothing completes** for "hireara alternative", "branded cv template", "recruitment fee protection" or "client went direct to candidate" [V].
- **Who ranks now.**
  - Commercial searches are won by vendor-written listicles, e.g. [RefineCV](https://refine-cv.com/blog/best-cv-formatting-software-recruitment-agencies/) and [FormaCV](https://formacv.ai/blog/best-cv-formatting-tools). Venditas is on none of them [V].
  - "HireAra alternative" is won by directories. No strong vendor page exists [V].
  - "Anonymise CV tool free" is won by free no-signup tools: SmallRecruiter, HubbaDO, Digitas Anon CV [V].
- **Directories.**
  - G2 has a free vendor plan ([G2](https://sell.g2.com/plans)) [V].
  - Capterra and GetApp list only ATSs as Allsorter's alternatives, and there is no CV-formatting category [V].
  - Allsorter publishes 21 Q&amp;A pages aimed at AI answers ([sitemap](https://allsorter.com/sitemap)) [V].
- **What AI answers cite.**
  - Vendor pages get 12–24% of B2B citations; G2, Capterra and Gartner together get 7.3% ([Growfusely](https://growfusely.com/blog/ai-citation-analysis-b2b-software/)) [2H].
  - Reddit, YouTube and LinkedIn are the most-cited domains overall ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-engines-cite-reddit-youtube-and-linkedin-most-study-473138)) [2H].
